About

A career built at the intersection of finance, strategy, and storytelling.

Charlie Dombek is a CPA, MBA, and internationally recognized wealth strategist with more than 30 years of experience helping affluent families and business owners protect and grow their wealth.

He began his career at Ernst & Young, one of the Big Four accounting firms, and holds an MBA from the College of William & Mary and a BS in Accounting from Virginia Tech.

Charlie is the founder of Axium Wealth, a tax advisory platform dedicated to wealth acceleration and tax mitigation, and a founding partner of Optimal Financial Group, a boutique CPA firm with a national presence. He has raised $300 million in equity for real estate projects valued at nearly $1 billion and has overseen a FOREX trading platform managing over $100 million in capital.
