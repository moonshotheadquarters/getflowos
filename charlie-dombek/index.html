About Charlie Dombek

A career built at the intersection of finance, strategy, and storytelling.

Charlie Dombek is a Certified Public Accountant (CPA), MBA, and internationally recognized wealth strategist with more than 30 years of experience helping affluent families, business owners, and high-net-worth individuals protect and grow their wealth through advanced tax planning and strategic financial advisory.

He began his career at Ernst & Young, one of the Big Four accounting firms, and holds an MBA from the College of William & Mary and a BS in Accounting from Virginia Tech. His clients include family offices, professional athletes, entertainers, and successful healthcare professionals.

Charlie is the founder of Axium Wealth, a tax advisory platform dedicated to wealth acceleration and tax mitigation; a founding partner of Optimal Financial Group, a boutique CPA firm with a national presence; and the founder of Dentistry Wealth, serving dental professionals nationwide.

Tax Strategy & Mitigation

Real Estate & Alternative Investments

About Charlie Dombek

Who is Charlie Dombek?

Charlie Dombek is a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) and MBA with over 30 years of experience as a global wealth strategist. He is the founder of Axium Wealth, Optimal Financial Group, and Dentistry Wealth. He began his career at Ernst & Young and is also an executive film producer with 50+ feature film credits.

What is Axium Wealth?

Axium Wealth is a tax advisory and wealth acceleration platform founded by Charlie Dombek. The firm specializes in advanced tax planning, estate planning, tax mitigation strategies, and wealth acceleration for affluent families, business owners, professional athletes, entertainers, and healthcare professionals.

What are Charlie Dombek's qualifications and credentials?

Charlie holds a CPA license, an MBA from the College of William & Mary, and a Bachelor of Science in Accounting from Virginia Tech. He is an Ernst & Young alumnus and a member of the AICPA. He has over three decades of experience in wealth strategy, tax planning, and financial advisory.

What tax planning and wealth services does Charlie Dombek provide?

Charlie specializes in advanced tax strategy and mitigation, real estate and alternative investments (including 1031 exchanges and Qualified Opportunity Zone Funds), estate and wealth planning, business owner advisory, and investment performance optimization. His compliance-first approach ensures all strategies are defensible and transparent.
