About

A Wealth Architect With a Unique Perspective

Charlie Dombek, CPA, MBA, is a highly regarded financial expert and tax strategist with an international reputation for creating innovative solutions in wealth management. With more than 30 years of experience as a practicing CPA, he has helped clients save significant amounts in taxes while building long-term financial stability.

Charlie began his career at Ernst & Young, one of the Big Four accounting firms. He holds an MBA from the College of William & Mary and a Bachelor of Science in Accounting from Virginia Tech.

He is the founder of Axium Wealth, a tax advisory platform serving affluent families and business owners, as well as a founding partner of Optimal Financial Group, a boutique CPA firm with a national presence.
